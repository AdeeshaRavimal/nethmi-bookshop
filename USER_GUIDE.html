1. Everyday POS & Customer Sales CASHIER

The Point of Sale (POS) screen is the heart of your daily store operations. Follow these simple steps for fast and error-free sales processing.

Step-by-Step Checkout Process

1
Scan or Search the Product
Point the barcode scanner at the product. If an item doesn't have a barcode (e.g. exercise books or loose stationery), press F1 and type the first 2-3 letters of the item name or SKU code. Press Enter to add it to the cart.
2
Adjust Quantity or Apply Discount (If needed)
Click + or - to adjust quantities, or type the exact quantity directly. To give a discount, click the discount button on the item line or at the bottom of the bill for a total order discount (percentage or fixed rupees).
3
Attach Customer (Optional for Loyalty & Credit)
Press F6 to search for an existing customer or enter their mobile phone number. Attaching a customer allows sending automated WhatsApp e-bills and tracking credit accounts.
4
Choose Payment Method & Complete Bill
Select how the customer is paying:
  • CASH (F2): Enter the cash amount handed by customer. The screen displays the exact change to return.
  • CARD (F3): Swipe/tap the card on your bank terminal, then confirm.
  • CUSTOMER CREDIT: Charges to customer's account (requires attached customer with available credit balance).
5
Bill Printing (A5 Portrait Format)
The system generates a clean, executive A5 Portrait Cash Bill / Tax Invoice. If the customer does not want a paper slip, simply close the print dialog or send the invoice to their phone via the green WhatsApp button.

Customer Returns & Exchanges

🔄 How to Handle a Return

1. Go to Bills from the sidebar.
2. Enter the Receipt Number from the customer's bill.
3. Select Process Return.
4. Choose items returned and reason (Damage / Wrong Item).
5. System automatically restocks inventory and generates an official A5 Return Voucher / Credit Note.

⚠️ Return Rules & Safety
Only Cashiers with return privileges or Managers (via PIN) can approve refunds. This prevents fraudulent duplicate returns and preserves inventory accuracy.

Daily Shift Close & Z-Report (Drawer Reconciliation)

At the end of every business day or cashier shift, the physical cash in the till must be reconciled against system recorded transactions.

1
Open Cashbook & Click "Daily Shift Close & Z-Report"
Navigate to Cashbook on the left menu, then click the blue "Daily Shift Close & Z-Report" button at top right.
2
Count Physical Notes Using the Denomination Calculator
Count every note and coin in your drawer and type the count:
Rs. 5,000 Notes Rs. 1,000 Notes Rs. 500 Notes Rs. 100 Notes Rs. 50 Notes Rs. 20 Notes Total Loose Coins Total Counted
3
Review Drawer Variance (Balanced / Surplus / Shortage)
The system compares Expected Cash (Opening + Cash Sales + Debt Collections - Supplier Payments - Petty Cash) against your Actual Count:
  • Balanced (Rs. 0.00): Perfect match.
  • Surplus (+Rs): More cash in drawer than recorded (e.g. unrecorded tip or customer forgot change).
  • Shortage (-Rs): Less cash in drawer (e.g. wrong change given or unrecorded cash payout).
4
Click "Save & Print Z-Report"
Saves the audit snapshot into permanent records and prints an official A5 Portrait 2-Column Z-Report. Both the Cashier and Store Owner must sign the printed slip and attach it to the cash bag.

3. Customer Credit & Stock Control ACCOUNTS & STOCK

Managing Customer Credit (Debt Book)

Many local schools, teachers, offices, and regular families purchase books and photocopying on monthly credit. Hiru Bookshop keeps an automated ledger for each customer.

💳 Setting Up a Credit Account

1. Go to Credit Customers.
2. Click + Add New Customer.
3. Enter Full Name, Mobile Phone, NIC (optional), and Credit Limit (Rs.).
4. Setting a Credit Limit (e.g. Rs. 15,000) prevents staff from issuing unauthorized debt beyond safe boundaries.

💵 Recording Customer Payments

1. When customer arrives to pay off debt, open Credit Customers.
2. Click Record Payment next to their name.
3. Enter amount paid (Cash or Bank Transfer) & notes.
4. The system automatically reduces their balance, records cash into today's drawer, and prints an official receipt.

⚠️ Credit Limit Exceeded Warning
If a customer attempts to buy items exceeding their assigned credit limit, the POS displays a prominent alert. Cashiers cannot complete the credit transaction unless approved by the Store Owner with Manager PIN.

Inventory & Product Management

Accurate stock tracking prevents lost inventory and ensures books and stationery are reordered before running out.

A
Adding a New Product
Go to Inventory → Click + Add Product. Scan or enter the Barcode/SKU, Title, Category (e.g. Stationery, Books, Office, Communication), Cost Price (Rs.), Selling Price (Rs.), and Alert Quantity.
B
Generating Barcode Stickers for Loose Items
For pens, rulers, or custom school packages that don't have factory barcodes, click the Barcode Generator icon. You can print sheet labels or single barcode stickers to attach to shelves or products.
C
FIFO Batch Cost Tracking
When prices change from suppliers, old stock retains the old wholesale cost, and new stock uses the new cost. The system automatically uses FIFO (First-In, First-Out) so your gross profit calculations are 100% accurate.

4. Goods Received Note (GRN) & Suppliers PURCHASING

When stock deliveries arrive from stationery distributors or publishers, they must be received into the system using a Goods Received Note (GRN).

How to Enter a Supplier Delivery (GRN)

1
Go to GRN in Sidebar & Select Supplier
Click GRN on the sidebar. Choose the supplier from the dropdown (e.g. Atlas, Richard, PrintX) and type their paper invoice number and invoice date.
2
Scan or Add Delivered Items
Scan each incoming product. Enter the received quantity, cost price per unit, and new selling price if retail price has changed.
3
Set Payment Terms: Cash Paid vs Supplier Credit
If you paid the delivery driver cash immediately, enter the amount in Paid Amount. If the delivery is on credit (payable in 30 days), enter 0. The remaining balance automatically moves to Supplier Payables.
4
Submit GRN
Click Save GRN. Stock quantities immediately update in the POS, a new cost batch is created, and the supplier payable balance is recorded.

Managing Supplier Payables

Task Description Action in System
Check Outstanding Dues See how much money Hiru Bookshop owes each supplier. Go to Suppliers → Review Total Payable column.
Record Cheque / Cash Payment Record payment made to distributor rep or bank deposit. Click Pay Supplier → Enter amount & cheque number.
Print Supplier Statement Print official reconciliation statement to verify against supplier bill. Click Print Statement on supplier profile page.
✅ Cash Flow Safe Guard
Whenever cash is paid to a supplier from the retail counter, recording it under "Pay Supplier" automatically records an expense in today's Cashbook, so your end-of-day drawer count matches perfectly!

The Cashbook provides a real-time ledger of all store money movements, preventing unexplained cash leakages.

Daily Cashbook Ledger Operations

6. Security, Backups & Troubleshooting SECURITY & CARE

Cashier Mode vs Manager PIN

To protect store profits and prevent unauthorized price tampering or bill voids, the system has built-in role protection:

🔒 What Cashier Mode Restricts
  • Hides Wholesale Cost Price & Gross Profit Margins
  • Prevents deleting or voiding paid sales receipts
  • Restricts modifying past audit records
  • Locks financial configuration and database settings
🔑 Unlocking with Manager PIN

Whenever a restricted action is triggered, a modal requests the 4-Digit Manager PIN. Once entered, the manager action proceeds safely.

Remote Authorization with Google Authenticator (TOTP)

📱 What if the Owner is away from the shop?
If the store owner is traveling or away from the shop and a cashier urgently needs to void a mistake, the owner can open Google Authenticator on their smartphone and read out the 6-digit Emergency Code over a phone call. The code rotates every 30 seconds for maximum security!

💾 1-Click Database Backup (Essential Routine)

Protect your store data against computer hardware failure or accidental power cuts by taking regular backups:

1
Click "Backup & Restore" in the Sidebar
Open the Database Management modal.
2
Click "Create Backup Now"
Save the generated .sqlite file onto a dedicated USB Pen Drive or your Google Drive folder. Take a backup every Saturday afternoon.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (Troubleshooting)

Issue / Question Recommended Solution
Barcode scanner doesn't enter text into POS Click inside the Search Box or press F1 to ensure cursor focus before scanning. Verify scanner USB cable is firmly connected.
Printer prints in wrong size In the Print Preview window, ensure Paper Size is set to A5 for customer bills and A4 for reports.
Cash drawer has a shortage at night Check if a petty cash expense (tea/supplies) was paid out from the drawer without clicking "Record Expense" in the Cashbook.
How to update to the latest version Click Check for Updates in the sidebar. If an update is found, the system will download and install it in one click.
